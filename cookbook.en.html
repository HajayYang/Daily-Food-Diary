🍅 Tomato and egg stir-fry

2 servings · 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • Tomato · 300 g
  • Eggs · 2 pcs
  • Cooking oil · 8 g
  • Salt · 1 g
  • Water · 30 ml

Method

  1. Wash and chop the tomatoes; beat the eggs. Amounts refer to raw edible portions.
  2. Heat half the oil over medium heat. Scramble the eggs until set, then remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil and tomatoes. Stir-fry for 3 minutes, add water and simmer for 2 minutes until juicy.
  4. Return the eggs, add salt and toss. Serve once all egg is fully set.

Meal pairing tip · Serve with mixed-grain rice and leafy greens. Tomatoes add sweetness without extra sugar.

🥔 Tangy shredded potatoes

2 servings · 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • Potato · 300 g
  • Garlic · 2 cloves
  • Rice vinegar · 10 ml
  • Fresh chili (optional) · 5 g
  • Cooking oil · 8 g
  • Salt · 1 g

Method

  1. Peel and shred the potatoes. Rinse off surface starch and drain; slice the garlic and chili.
  2. Heat the oil over medium heat; briefly fry the garlic and optional chili without burning.
  3. Add the potatoes and stir-fry over medium-high heat for 4–6 minutes. Add a little water if they stick.
  4. Add vinegar and salt; serve when the potatoes have no hard raw center.

Meal pairing tip · Count potatoes toward the starchy part of the meal; serve less rice and add tofu or lean meat.

🐟 Ginger steamed fish

2 servings · 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • Boneless fish fillet · 300 g
  • Ginger · 10 g
  • Scallion · 10 g
  • Light soy sauce (check label) · 5 ml
  • Cooking oil · 4 g

Method

  1. Pat thawed fish dry and check for bones; place on a heatproof plate. Slice ginger and scallion.
  2. Top fish with ginger and half the scallion; bring water in the steamer to a boil.
  3. Steam over high heat for about 8–12 minutes, longer for thicker pieces. Check the center reaches 63°C and flakes easily.
  4. Carefully drain some cooking liquid, drizzle with soy sauce and oil, and add scallion. Watch for bones when eating.

Meal pairing tip · Serve with rice and two vegetables. Choose another recipe if allergic to fish or ingredients in soy sauce.

🥚 Savory steamed eggs

2 servings · 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • Eggs · 2 pcs
  • Water · 150 ml
  • Salt · 0.5 g
  • Cooking oil · 2 g
  • Scallion · 5 g

Method

  1. Beat eggs, then mix in salt and lukewarm water that is not hot to the touch.
  2. Strain into a heatproof bowl, skim bubbles and cover with a heatproof plate to prevent drips.
  3. Place in a boiling steamer; steam over medium-low heat for 10–15 minutes, longer for deeper or larger portions.
  4. Check the center is fully set with no liquid egg; remove and top with oil and washed scallion.

Meal pairing tip · Eggs provide protein; add vegetables and a staple for a complete meal.

  • Commercial red rice wine lees · 30 g
  • Ginger · 15 g
  • Light soy sauce (check label) · 8 ml
  • Cooking oil · 10 g
  • Water · 250 ml

Method
