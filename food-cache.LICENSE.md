# Open Food Facts snapshot attribution

`food-cache.json` contains a limited extraction from the Open Food Facts production database, retrieved through <https://search.openfoodfacts.org/search>.

This dataset is provided under ODbL, separately from the application's MIT-licensed code. Retain attribution and comply with applicable ODbL sharing requirements when redistributing the dataset or derivative databases.

Individual products link to their original `https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/{code}` pages. Retrieval times are recorded per query group. This is neither a full database nor a guarantee that a label remains current; verify the actual packaging. Unknown values remain unknown.
